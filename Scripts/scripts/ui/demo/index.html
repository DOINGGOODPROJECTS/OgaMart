OgaMart UI kit – live demo

Everything here is built only from the kit PNGs and moved by scripts/ogamart_ui.js, the same file you paste into GDevelop.

Try it:

  • Restock: the stock bar fills and coins count down
  • Every few seconds a customer buys bread: +5 floats up, coins and XP bars move, and at full XP you get a level up with confetti and a toast
  • Tap the nav tabs: the gold highlight slides
  • Tap the settings button: a sheet slides up with a slider, a switch and a segmented control
  • The red ! pulses until you restock
