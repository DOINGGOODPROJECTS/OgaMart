Inter (rsms.me/inter) and Poppins (Indian Type Foundry, fonts.google.com/specimen/Poppins) are free fonts released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (openfontlicense.org).
You can use them in your game, including commercial games. Do not sell the font files on their own.
Poppins ExtraBold (used for a few big titles) is not included: Poppins Bold is the closest match here, or download Poppins ExtraBold free from Google Fonts.
